# Belgium → Portugal: seller-country side

> Snapshot: researched Oct–Nov 2026. Prices, laws and tool behaviour drift — re-check anything you act on.

Tags: **V** verified (official/primary) · **A** anecdotal/forum/commercial · **I** inferred. The BE part applies to any destination except where it says PT (proof of PT residence, PT insurer, the 30-day window vs PT's inspection). Destination side: [pt-registration.md](pt-registration.md). Payment: [../buy/deposit-payment.md](../buy/deposit-payment.md).

**Difficulty: moderate.** Belgian plates belong to the owner and **leave with the seller**, so the car has no plate after the sale. You need half a day in Brussels for the **X export plate** (30 days, **no extension**), which must cover the drive **and** the PT inspection.

## 1. Documents to collect (originals)

| Document | Rule | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Registration certificate (CIM), both parts** | Two-part CIM since 2013 (one in the car, one at home). No transfer code. It "suit le véhicule" but is **not proof of ownership**. Lost part → police declaration + duplicate (police attestation accepted for the X plate) | V ([mobilit CIM, chg 06/05/2026](https://mobilit.belgium.be/fr/route/immatriculer/plaques-et-certificats-dimmatriculation/certificat-dimmatriculation)) |
| **Car-Pass** | **Mandatory** unless the buyer is a dealer — explicitly also for a foreign private buyer. **≤2 months old**; buyer signs for a copy. **€11.10** (free if <4 readings). Missing → "the sale is invalid"; the buyer can have it annulled and recover the price (but from abroad that means a Belgian court case). Verify by QR code or ID on car-pass.be | V ([car-pass.be](https://www.car-pass.be/en/private-individuals/i-want-to-sell-a-used-vehicle/you-are-obliged-to-provide-a-car-pass-to-the-buyer)) |
| Battery SoH | Car-Pass shows SoH from 2026 but **optional** (letter of intent, no law). Other routes: brand dealer, VAB, Aviloo → [../battery-soh.md](../battery-soh.md) | V |
| **CoC** | Not on the DIV X-plate list, but **PT needs it** (IMT €45 vs €165). VW-group duplicate from the Belgian importer: **€160**, lead time unstated — get it before flying | V |
| **Sales contract**, 2 copies | No official form. Both parties, VIN, km, date + time, price, **Car-Pass number with buyer's acknowledgement**; **names the buyer** (PT DAV upload). Templates: police model; AutoScout24 BE private-sale PDF | V |
| Original purchase invoice + proof not financed/leased | Practical proof of free title (see §5) | I |
| Service book/invoices, all keys + key code | Consumer-org handover list | A |

**No inspection for the sale or export** if the car is <4 years old and still will be at plate expiry ("pas de contrôle technique"). The second-hand inspection is for Belgian re-registration (Flanders abolishes it for domestic sales from 1 Jan 2027; Wallonia/Brussels keep it) — irrelevant to export. Some agents ask for a CT valid 31+ days; that conflicts with the official rule. **V**

## 2. Who de-registers, and when

- **The plate never stays on a sold car** ("Ne jamais laisser la plaque sur un véhicule vendu"). The seller unscrews it at the handover. **V**
- The seller has **4 months** to cancel the plate (post to DIV or drop at Rue du Progrès 56) or move it to his next car; after that the DIV cancels automatically. Since 1 Aug 2025 only the DIV can cancel. His tax/insurance problem, not the buyer's. **V**
- No Belgian registration tax (BIV/TMC) for the buyer on a truck export; Flanders exempts cars exported within 6 months. Whether an X-plate registration triggers BIV is **unverified** (small for a BEV anyway, Flanders min. ≈ €61.50). **V/I**

## 3. Transit: X plate (default), or truck

**X plate ("plaque X", transit/export)** — [mobilit.belgium.be, chg 08/01/2026](https://mobilit.belgium.be/fr/route/immatriculer/exporter-un-vehicule-plaque-x) (plain fetch hits a captcha; read in a browser)
- **Who:** people not on the Belgian National/Waiting Register, living abroad, who bought a car in Belgium to export it. **V**
- **Where:** DIV "TRANSIT" counter, City Atrium, Rue du Progrès 56–60, 1210 Brussels, 8:30–13:00 and 14:00–15:30, weekdays. No appointment system mentioned (probably walk-in). Closed on Belgian public holidays (e.g. 11 Nov). **V/I**
- **Proxy allowed** ("mandataire (ex : agence en douane) ou votre assureur"). Agents near Rue Picard, 1080 Brussels sell the insurance, fill in the form and queue. Whether the buyer must attend himself is **unverified** (his original ID is on the list). **V/I**
- **Originals only:** registration form with the insurer's **yellow vignette** (tick X1 "transit", X2 "véhicule usagé"; Belgian contact address — an agent's is OK; destination address in box X10) · green card · seller's **last CIM** (or police attestation) · sales contract · passport/ID · **original proof of residence abroad if the ID shows no address** — a PT CC/residence card shows none, so bring an **atestado de residência** from the Junta de Freguesia (PT-specific, **I**). **V**
- Not required: CT (<4 years), Car-Pass, CoC; the car need not be present (**I**: not mentioned).
- **Cost/timing:** **€75** plate. File before ~12:00–13:00 (agents say arrive before 10:00) → collect **same day 17:00–18:00 at bpost Bruxelles De Brouckère** or at the agent's office. The €80 rush delivery doesn't apply to X plates. **V** (fees page chg 08/09/2025)
- **Validity: 30 calendar days, no extension** (an extension = new plate, another €75 and visit). 4 months applies only to VAT-exempt dealer exports. Once a car has had an X plate it can't get another temporary plate under a different holder; misuse → 5-year ban. **V** (validity table 06/12/2022)
- Dealer Z/ZZ and trial plates: professionals only. The 20-day national plate is Belgium-only — useless. **V**

**Insurance for the X plate**
- Sold by the Brussels agents, who stick the vignette on the spot; "toutes les compagnies ne proposent pas d'assurance temporaire" (DIV). **V**
- Price not published: ≈ **€220–320 plate + insurance** (agent and comparison sites). **A**
- **Third-party (RC) only** — no casco found, so **theft/damage of the car itself is uninsured on the drive**. A Belgian RC certificate must cover the whole EU/EEA (AR 16/04/2018 art. 4, 39); check no country is crossed out on the green card. **V/A**
- A PT insurer instead: an older DIV text allowed an insurer "with its seat in the destination EU country"; **the current page no longer says so**, and no PT product for an X plate was found. Treat as unavailable. French insurers usually won't cover a Belgian transit plate. **A**
- "Assurance frontière" is for non-green-card cars — doesn't apply. **V**

**Don't drive on the seller's plate.** The standard RC contract keeps third-party cover 16 days after a sale "même illicitement", but the insurer has **recourse against the driver** after an ownership change (AR 2018 annex art. 10 §1, art. 48 — annex text partly unverified). Illegal, buyer carries the damage, seller gets the fines. **V/A**

**PT plate before leaving:** doesn't exist. **V**

**Truck:** car loaded at the seller's (no plate needed, **I**). Shared open truck ≈ **€900 + VAT ≈ €1,107**, enclosed €1,300–1,400, 8–12 days, full-value cover (excess €250–500); marketplace jobs €1,200–1,280; some carriers cap cover at €25,000 — too low for a typical used EV. Pickup 2–5 weeks. Keep the **CMR** (condition + PT entry proof). Car arrives plateless: deliver to the inspection centre or tow. Self-towing on a rental trailer is out (typical rental car trailer 1,960 kg max vs ~2,000–2,150 kg for a mid-size BEV SUV). **A** (prices Sep 2026)

## 4. VAT

- Used = >6 months **and** >6,000 km (BE Code TVA art. 8bis §2 2°, circular 2024/C/47; PT RITI art. 6). Check **field B** on the CIM for any car under a year old. New → PT IVA 23% on the DAV; the BE seller files form 447. **V**
- Private seller, used: no VAT. A company/lessor invoice with BE VAT (21%) is final for a private buyer; no PT VAT. **V**

## 5. BE-specific traps

1. **Company or lease car.** The CIM is not proof of ownership: a financed/leased car is registered to the **user**. Fleet trims ("Pro Business"-style in the VW range, 2026 example) are often company/lease cars. If C.1 ≠ seller, or he can't show the original invoice in his name, he can't sell it — buy from the lessor/employer (their invoice) or walk away. A "private" ad photographed on a brand-dealer lot is a tell. **V/A**
2. **No Belgian non-gage certificate.** The pledge register (Pandregister, SPF Finances) needs a **Belgian eID/itsme** to search and registration is optional — the seller can search and show the result; incomplete. Proof of free title = original invoice in the seller's name + lender's payoff/release letter for a loan + C.1 matching the seller's ID. **V/I**
3. **Trader on a private account:** ask for an invoice; a trader owes the legal guarantee and a Car-Pass, may charge margin-scheme VAT (fine). **A**
4. **Odometer fraud** concentrates in **cars imported used**: Car-Pass 2025: 1,466 fraud cases, 543 on used imports, avg rollback ~80k km. A Car-Pass history starts only at the Belgian import inspection — a short history on an older car is a flag. **V**
5. No Car-Pass / >2 months old / km mismatch → insist on a fresh one; check against the odometer and service history.
6. **Missing CoC:** €160 duplicate, unknown lead time; PT IMT €165 instead of €45.
7. **30 days, no extension:** without a pre-booked PT inspection the car ends up stranded plateless (tow). A Friday arrival, a holiday or a queue past ~12:00 costs a night.
8. **RC-only cover on the drive** — if that matters, truck it.
9. **Scams:** fake ads asking half up front to a foreign IBAN with "delivery by transport company"; fake transfer screenshots shown to sellers (2024, Mar 2026 press). Pay SEPA Instant at the car with Verification of Payee (Belgian banks went live first in the eurozone). Cash private→private is legal (the €3,000 limit is for payments to traders) but carrying it is the risk. No standard escrow; a 2019 marketplace escrow partnership is not confirmed active. Deposit: write **acompte** (binds the sale) or **arrhes** (either side may withdraw: buyer loses it, seller repays double) explicitly — forum source, new Civil Code Book 7 unchecked. **V/A**
10. No public stolen-car check: match the stamped VIN with CoC, CIM and plates; ask the DIV if in doubt. **V**
11. LEZ (Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent) require foreign-plated cars to register (free); a BEV meets the criteria; X-plate recognition probably automatic **I**.

## 7. Buyer step list

**Before (remote)**
1. Seller sends scans: CIM both parts (C.1 = seller, field B date), ID, CoC, original purchase invoice, service history, SoH report if any, loan payoff letter if financed.
2. Seller orders a fresh Car-Pass (≤2 months old on the sale day), ideally with SoH.
3. Agree the contract (buyer named with PT address) and payment: SEPA Instant at handover, no deposit (or a small one explicitly called *acompte*).
4. PT prep: IMT homologation online; **book the PT Category B inspection** ~4–10 days after return, well inside the 30 days.
5. Get an original *atestado de residência* (Junta de Freguesia). Fly in Mon–Thu, not on a Belgian holiday.

**In Belgium (2 days, or one long day)**
6. Day 1: handover — inspect, test-drive, VIN on car = CIM = CoC, Car-Pass km = odometer, verify the Car-Pass QR, sign 2 contracts + the Car-Pass, pay (VoP match), take all originals + both keys. Seller unscrews his plate; the car stays parked at his place. (Paying before the plate exists is the normal order — the DIV needs the original CIM and signed contract.)
7. Day 2, 08:30–11:00: agent → X-plate insurance + vignette + form → DIV TRANSIT counter before ~11:00.
8. Day 2, 15:15–18:00: collect the plate (agent office or bpost De Brouckère), return to the car, fit plates.
9. Days 3–5: drive ~2,000 km (~20 h, ~8–9 DC stops).

**In PT:** inspection **before day 30**, then DAV → plates + PT insurance → IMT (original Belgian CIM) → IRN ([pt-registration.md](pt-registration.md)).
