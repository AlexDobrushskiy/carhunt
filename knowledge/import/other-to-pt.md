# Other seller countries → Portugal (LU, NL, IT; AT and the rest not researched)

## Luxembourg

**Documents and de-registration** **V** ([SNCA, selling](https://snca.public.lu/en/vehicules/vendre-hors-circulation/vendre-vehicule.html), [guichet.lu](https://guichet.public.lu/fr/citoyens/transport/transports-individuels/vehicule-motorise/vendre-retirer-vehicule/vendre-vehicule-luxembourg.html))
- The seller notifies the SNCA within **5 working days** and asks for an **export attestation (€19.80)** — MyGuichet (private individuals only), counter or post.
- The buyer gets **both certificate parts stamped "vehicle not registered"**; the seller removes the plates.
- If the car had a VAT or customs exemption, proof its status is in order is needed.
- Also collect the CoC (PT needs it) and, from a company seller, a Trade Register extract.

**Transit: LU export plates** **V** ([SNCA export plates](https://snca.public.lu/en/plaques/exportation.html))
- Open to buyers living abroad if the last owner has an official LU address (a company qualifies).
- Steps: export number from the SNCA (bring contract/invoice) → insure with an **LU-approved insurer** → registration → plates from one of 3 plate makers.
- **In person at SNCA Sandweiler only, ~1 full day**, Mon–Fri 07:30–16:30.
- Valid **until the end of the 3rd month after the current month** — comfortably covers the drive + PT inspection.
- SNCA fee and insurance price not published. **A**

**Company seller, "TVA récupérable"** — common on LU ads
- Means ordinary VAT on the invoice (LU 17%). A private buyer pays it inside the price and **cannot reclaim it**; zero-rating is for VAT-registered businesses or new vehicles. A used car (>6 months **and** >6,000 km) owes **no PT VAT** on arrival. **V** (Your Europe, EC Taxation)
- **I:** if the seller arranges transport to PT, it may become a distance sale taxed at PT 23%. Collect it or truck it on your own contract; check the invoice wording.
- Watch for "net export" prices quoted to businesses only.

**Costs/days:** ~2,000 km to PT, same pattern as DE (2 nights). Truck BE/LU ≈ €900 + VAT **A**.

**Steps:** before — invoice draft, both certificate parts, CoC, Trade Register extract, pre-arrange the LU insurer, book the PT inspection · day 1 🏢 SNCA Sandweiler + plate maker · days 2–3 drive.

## Netherlands

**Documents and de-registration** **V** ([RDW export](https://www.rdw.nl/invoeren-exporteren-doorvoeren/voertuig-exporteren), [NederlandWereldwijd](https://www.nederlandwereldwijd.nl/auto-exporteren/stappenplan))
- Seller hands over the **kentekencard + tenaamstellingscode** (registration card + transfer code).
- RDW advises the **buyer exports in his own name** (buyer's details on the export document); otherwise the seller stays responsible until the car is registered abroad.
- At an RDW inspection station/desk or a licensed export company 🏢, bring ID + card + code + plates: **€12 at RDW, walk-in**.
- Output: **vrijwaringsbewijs** (proof NL registration ended) + kentekencard with a corner cut off + **exportbewijs (kentekenbewijs deel II)** — needed to register in PT.
- Online "Melden export" is only for cars already abroad.
- Pre-purchase: RDW open data by plate is free with no key (import date, odometer verdict, open recall) — `carhunt vin rdw PLATE`; the owner can get the RDW-Voertuigrapport free and forward it → [../vin-sources.md](../vin-sources.md).

**Transit**
- After de-registration: **14 days, in NL only**, on a white export plate with black characters, with valid APK (roadworthiness) + temporary insurance. Driving abroad on it depends on each country; a transit plate from the destination or first country entered is advised. **V**
- A German export plate for a foreign-registered car needs the car presented in DE, a German HU, CoC and an EBV — clumsy. **V** (Berlin office page)
- → **Truck it** (NL ≈ €950 + VAT **A**) and deliver to the PT inspection centre, or accept the risk of driving BE/FR/ES on the white plate. Recognition of the white plate in BE/FR/ES is **unverified**.

**Money:** the NL register is not a lien register either. Pay as DE (SEPA Instant + Verification of Payee).

**Steps:** before — kentekencard photo, APK date, CoC, seller confirms he'll hand over the code · day 1 🏢 RDW station (walk-in) → white plate → truck pickup.

## Italy

All from a secondary guide (SicurAUTO) unless noted — **A**.
- **Documents:** since 2020 one combined registration + title, the **Documento Unico (DU)**.
- **Avoid the passaggio di proprietà** (title transfer). The **radiazione per esportazione** can be requested by the owner **or "un acquirente non ancora intestatario"** with the original purchase deed.
- **Where:** book on PrenotACI → ACI/PRA counter (STA), or an agenzia pratiche auto.
- **Bring:** Istanza Unificata, DU, **both plates**, ID + **codice fiscale** (Italian tax code — a foreign buyer usually lacks one → in practice the seller files). **I**
- **Cost:** ACI €13.50 + stamp duty €32 + €10.20 + postage ≈ **€60**; an agency adds €50–100.
- **Must be filed BEFORE the car leaves Italy** (CdS art. 103, L. 120/2020); late filing costs €173–694 plus proof of foreign registration.
- **Output:** DU marked "cessato dalla circolazione"; plates surrendered → driving needs a **"foglio di via" + temporary plate from the Motorizzazione** (fee and same-day issue unknown). **Targa EE** (foreign-tourist plates, max 1 year, authorised Motorizzazione offices, customs/consular residence declaration) is heavier; insurance prices not found.
- **Blocks:** a fermo amministrativo must be cleared; liens/attachments need the creditor's consent.
- **Verdict:** harder than ES. **Truck is the default**; have it delivered to the PT inspection centre.

## Any other country (AT, PL, CZ, …): generic checklist

Not researched — build the answers before committing, from the country's official registry pages (tag **V/A/I**), then consider writing a proper `<cc>-to-pt.md`:
1. Which document(s) prove registration and title, and does the buyer get the **originals** (PT IMT keeps them)?
2. Who de-registers, online or counter, and can it wait until the money lands?
3. Is there an **export/transit plate valid abroad for ≥30 days** with insurance (drive + PT inspection)? Who may apply (foreign address, local recipient, appointment, opening hours)?
4. Is a national short-term plate refused in transit countries? (DE's 5-day plate is refused in FR/BE/NL/LU.)
5. How is a lien/lease/company ownership checked (register, title held by lender, field naming a lessor)?
6. Does a title transfer trigger a local transfer tax (ES ~6%)? Avoid transfers.
7. Is a roadworthiness test or odometer certificate mandatory for the sale?
8. If no plate route ≥30 days exists: **truck it** and deliver to the PT inspection centre.
