# Spain → Portugal: seller-country side

> Snapshot: researched Oct–Nov 2026 (EV run). Prices, laws and tool behaviour drift — re-check anything you act on.

Tags: **V** verified (official/primary) · **A** anecdotal/forum/commercial · **I** inferred. The ES part applies to any destination; Route B's timing is tied to PT's order of steps ([pt-registration.md](pt-registration.md)). Payment: [../buy/deposit-payment.md](../buy/deposit-payment.md).

**Difficulty: moderate.** DGT fees ≈ €30 and mostly online, but **every DGT step needs a Spanish e-ID (Cl@ve/certificate) or DNI/NIE** — a foreign buyer usually has neither, so **the seller (or a gestoría for him) files**. Agree up front who files the export deregistration (baja) and when.

## 0. Recent rule changes **V** ([RGV, BOE](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1999-1826))

- Since 15 Apr 2021 (RD 265/2021): after a "baja por traslado a otro país" the car must leave Spain within **3 months**, or DGT re-registers it automatically (RGV art. 35.4).
- Same reform: **ITV pass within 30 days before the baja** unless recorded electronically — only for cars **>4 years** old (Anexo XV A 11º).
- RD 52/2026 (Jan 2026) renamed the register "Registro Nacional de Vehículos"; procedures unchanged.

## 1. Never take a title transfer

| | **(a) Baja definitiva por traslado a otro país** ✅ | (b) Transferencia, then baja ❌ |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | Can be requested by "titular o tercera persona que acredite su propiedad"; online the sale contract is the proof (RGV art. 35.4, DGT sede) **V** | — |
| Fee | **Tasa 4.1 = €8.67** (free if 15+ years) **V** | Transfer fee **+ ITP transfer tax paid by the buyer, ~6%** of the higher of price and Hacienda's table value (6% in the region checked; rate varies by autonomous community **I**). ≈ €2,100 on a €35k car **V** |
| Foreign buyer as applicant? | In person needs DNI, Spanish licence, residence card or passport + NIE; online needs Cl@ve → **in practice no** | Needs proof of an address in Spain (RGV Anexo XIII A 3º) → **not possible** for a non-resident **V** |

(EV run 2026-11-04: the EU portal page still describes transfer-then-export; it was last updated 2022.)

**How the baja works**
- **Online** (seller with Cl@ve/certificate or the miDGT app): upload the signed contract (**every page signed by all owners and the buyer**) + €8.67 → filing receipt (justificante), then an email when processed. **No published processing time.** **V** ([DGT sede](https://sede.dgt.gob.es/es/vehiculos/altas-bajas-y-rehabilitacion/baja-por-exportacion/))
- **In person:** Jefatura with appointment (cita previa): Mod. 01, ID, fee, original permiso + ITV card, Mod. 24 if a representative goes. The Jefatura **hands back the originals with the permiso marked "anulado"** — you need them for PT (RGV art. 37.1). **V**
- **Gestoría** files online for the seller, no appointment; **€80–150** (PT importer estimate) **A**.
- **Blocks:** precinto (seizure order); **reserva de dominio** (must be cancelled in the Registro de Bienes Muebles with the bank's carta de cancelación); any limitation on disposal. **V** Unpaid fines may block it **A**.
- After the baja the car **may not be driven** except on green plates or by truck. **V**
- ITP on route (a): no DGT step checks it; no source addresses a non-resident exporting buyer — residual paper risk only. **I**
- **What PT expects:** original permiso + ficha técnica + contract + proof of the baja ("sem ela não pode ser matriculado em Portugal" — **A**, PT importer). EU rule: the new state withdraws the old certificate (Dir. 1999/37 art. 5) **V**. Whether the DAV/IMT strictly requires the baja document is **unverified** — Route B covers both cases.

## 2. Documents to collect

| Doc | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Permiso de circulación** (original) | Names the registered owner; it is the PT registration input |
| **Ficha técnica — eITV** | Cars registered after May 2016 have an electronic card **printed on plain A4**. Lost → duplicate €8.67, ~2 weeks by post → **ask for the printout now** **V** |
| **Contrato de compraventa**, 2 copies | Plate, VIN, price, **date and time**, **signed on every page by every owner and the buyer**. Add "vendido para exportación a Portugal; el vendedor tramitará la baja por traslado". Names the buyer (PT DAV upload) **V** |
| Seller's DNI (copy) | Must match the owner on the permiso and the DGT report |
| **DGT informe completo** | Owner, ITV history, km, owners, liens/seizures, recalls. €8.67; seller gets it in miDGT or by phone (060). Verify its CSV code on the DGT verification page **V** |
| **CoC** | PT needs it (IMT €45 vs €165); seller or brand |
| Service book / dealer invoices | Main km evidence — a <4-year-old car has no ITV km records yet (first ITV at 4 years, RD 920/2017) |
| Last IVTM (road tax) receipt | On the in-person baja list only |
| Carta de cancelación de reserva de dominio | Only if financed |

The CERO/ECO environmental sticker is tied to the Spanish plate — irrelevant after export.

## 3. Transit routes

| Route | How | € in ES | Days |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B. Drive on Spanish plates; seller files the baja after the PT inspection** (recommended for PT) | Car stays registered and insured in Spain; under LCS art. 34 the **buyer takes over the policy at sale**, insurer can cancel only with 1 month's notice (art. 35) **V**. Seller files the baja online with the contract **after the PT Category B inspection, before the DAV**. One gestoría advises deregistering after foreign registration **A** | 8.67 + charging | same day |
| A. Baja + green plates (placas verdes), drive | Baja first, then temporary permit **60 days, tasa 1.4 €20.61** (RGV art. 43.1.2.2), plates from an authorised plate centre. Applicant: the owner or authorised person — **seller or gestoría applies**. Online the permit is **posted home** with no guaranteed time; same-day in person plausible but unconfirmed. Plan 1–3 days; seller files the baja ~1 week before **V/A** | ~€250–450 (fees + plates ~€20–40 + temp insurance ~€60–150 + gestoría) | 2–3 |
| C. Baja + truck | Seller files whenever; car collected at his door. Shared carrier €0.30–0.50/km, dedicated €0.60–1.20/km; ~€350–700 for ~1,000 km; 1–3 days transit + lead time **A** | ~€360–710 | buyer 1 day; car ~1–2 weeks |

**Route B — size the risks honestly**
- DGT says "realiza la baja **antes** de llevarte el vehículo" — Route B goes against DGT guidance. **V**
- Exposure: seller stays registered owner (speed-camera fines go to him; he names the buyer as driver); seller must trust the buyer and actually file afterwards; seller's insurer may cancel (1 month's notice); PT may want the baja document → seller files next day online (€8.67, 10 min).
- **Don't rely on the seller's policy transferring — buy your own insurance for the drive + inspection.** (EV run 2026-11-04)
- Mitigation: a signed declaration of responsibility from the time of sale, recorded in the contract.

**Insurance for green plates:** policy needs the green plate number first; example (small car, Madrid-resident driver) €24/1 day, €54–69/7 days — a BEV costs more; ID: DNI, NIE **or passport**; driver 21+, licence 1+ year; check Portugal is covered. **A**

## 5. ES-specific traps

- **Title transfer = ~6% ITP.** Never. Baja only.
- **Reserva de dominio** shows in the DGT report's liens section; baja impossible until cancelled. If the seller pays off the loan with your money, pay the bank directly and get the carta de cancelación — days, not one visit. **V/A**
- **Leasing/renting** is annotated on the register (RGV art. 37.4); registered owner a company or leasing firm → walk away unless there's a documented buyout. **V**
- DNI = owner on permiso and informe; VIN on car = permiso = eITV; co-owners all sign. **V**
- Free **informe reducido** by plate (first registration + blocks) — the buyer can run it himself; fake third-party "report" sites exist (one charged €12.70). **V/A**
- **Bizum useless** (max €1,000/transfer, €2,000/day). No deposits to "transport companies" the seller names (known marketplace scam pattern, Guardia Civil warning 2025). **A**
- Brand warranty may be limited to cars first sold by the Spanish network → [../coverage-rules.md](../coverage-rules.md).

## 6. Buyer step list

**Before (remote)**
1. Plate + VIN; run the free informe reducido.
2. From the seller: informe completo PDF (verify CSV), photos of permiso + eITV printout + DNI, CoC, service records, no-finance confirmation (or payoff letter).
3. Agree route + contract text (export clause, every page signed). Route B: seller commits in writing to file the baja after your PT inspection. Route A: seller/gestoría files the baja ~1 week before and books the Jefatura for green plates.
4. Fresh ITV only if the car is >4 years old.
5. Buy your own insurance for the drive + PT inspection; book the PT Category B inspection.

**Deal day**
6. Inspect, test-drive; VIN = documents, DNI = owner; battery evidence → [../battery-soh.md](../battery-soh.md).
7. Sign 2 contracts with date + time; SEPA Instant with Verification of Payee; collect permiso, eITV printout, CoC, keys/cards, contract.
8. Route B: drive. Route A: green plates → insurance with the plate number → drive. Route C: hand to the carrier.

**After**
9. Route B: after the PT inspection, seller files the baja online and forwards receipt + DGT confirmation email.
10. Keep all Spanish originals for the DAV/IMT ([pt-registration.md](pt-registration.md)).
