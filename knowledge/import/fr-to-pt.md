# France → Portugal: seller-country side

> Snapshot: researched Oct–Nov 2026 (EV run). Prices, laws and tool behaviour drift — re-check anything you act on.

Tags: **V** verified (official/primary) · **A** anecdotal/forum/commercial · **I** inferred. The FR part applies to any destination; insurance length is driven by the destination's order of steps — for PT see [pt-registration.md](pt-registration.md). Payment: [../buy/deposit-payment.md](../buy/deposit-payment.md).

**Difficulty: easy.** No office, no new plates, nothing for the buyer to file in France. One ~1 h handover, then drive home on the car's **existing French plates** with **insurance in the buyer's own name**.

## 1. Documents to collect (originals)

| Document | Rule | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| **Certificat d'immatriculation (carte grise), original** | Seller crosses it out, writes "vendu le" + **date + time**, signs, hands over the whole document. PT import can't be done without it — never a photocopy, never "posted later" | V ([service-public F1707](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1707)) / A (PT importer) |
| Coupon détachable | Seller fills in the buyer's name and signs, without tearing it off. Valid 1 month **in France only** ("pas valable pour circuler à l'étranger") | V (F1707, ANTS FAQ) |
| **Certificat de cession, Cerfa 15776**, 2 copies | Both fill in, with **date + time**; copy 2 is the buyer's; all co-owners sign | V |
| Code de cession (ANTS) | Valid 15 days; only needed to register the car in France — an exporting buyer can ignore it | V |
| **Certificat de situation administrative (CSA)** | From HistoVec, **<15 days old**; shows gage (lien), opposition, OTCI | V |
| Contrôle technique | Only for cars **>4 years** old (report <6 months). Not needed under 4 years | V |
| **CoC** | Recommended for export; PT needs it (IMT €45 vs €165). From the seller or the brand | V |
| Service book/invoices, both keys, charging cable | Not legal requirements; ask | — |

## 2. Who de-registers, and when

- **The seller**, online on ANTS ("Vendre ou donner mon véhicule"), **within 15 days**, with FranceConnect / France Identité / ANTS account. He enters the buyer's identity (birth name, date/place of birth, sex, email, postal address) and the mileage. Required "même si vous vendez … à l'étranger"; a foreign address is fine. **V** (F1707)
- **Buyer on ANTS: nothing.** "Changement de titulaire" is for registering in France (needs FranceConnect + French address). Simplimmat works only if both live in France. **V**
- No préfecture counters since 2017. **V**
- Ask for a screenshot/receipt of the ANTS cession for your file.

## 3. Transit: French plates + your own temporary insurance ≥3 weeks

- **The plate stays with the car for life (SIV).** Nothing is unscrewed or issued. **V**
- **WW plate: not available** for a car already on a definitive SIV number (arrêté 9 Feb 2009 art. 8, version in force since 1 Jan 2026: WW-for-export only for new cars, foreign-registered used cars bought from a French professional, or old pre-2009 FNI numbers). French customs: used cars are exported "sans changement d'immatriculation". **V** (Légifrance, douane.gouv.fr)
  - Since 1 Jan 2026 WW/W plates are pink with an expiry month; WW validity is 4 months (the old "1 month renewable once" page is out of date); W garage = professionals only. **V/A**
- **Legal status of the drive:** car still validly registered in France; carry originals of barred carte grise + Cerfa copy 2 + CSA + your insurance. Grey zone: the coupon is France-only, so abroad the papers prove ownership but are not a registration in your name. No rule found that forbids it; it is the standard export route. Risk low — at worst explaining at a roadside check. **A/I**
- **Liability** passes to the buyer at the date+time on the cession; French speed-camera tickets will reach you at home. **V** (F1480)

**Insurance — the one real to-do**
- **The seller's policy does not cover you.** Cover in your name from the handover, valid in FR, ES, PT. **V** (Your Europe)
- **Length: ≥3 weeks** — it must cover the drive **plus the PT Category B inspection** before the DAV, not the 3–7 days the insurers' examples quote. (EV run 2026-11-04)
- **Option A — French "assurance temporaire":** 1–90 days, whole EU. Example price (French plate, **Jul 2026**): **€71.77 / 3 days, €88.99 / 7 days** (liability + assistance); longer costs more. A cession certificate "suffit généralement" as proof. **V** (insurer pages). ⚠️ **Unverified whether they accept a PT-resident driver with a PT licence** — test it in the online quote. **I**
- **Option B — PT insurer from the purchase date on the French plate** (EU Dir. 2009/103 art. 15 allows 30 days). Commercial PT pages say most PT insurers accept the foreign registration and sell 1–28-day policies; no product page confirmed. **A** — see [pt-registration.md](pt-registration.md) §9.

**Truck instead:** €400–800, 4–7 days (PT importer); €900–1,300 (2026 comparison site); dedicated truck 5–10 days. You still fly in for the handover. **A**

## 5. FR-specific traps

- **HistoVec** (official, free, histovec.interieur.gouv.fr): only the **owner** can generate and share the report; the buyer can't pull it by plate. Shows first registration, owner changes, **VE/VGE write-offs**, gage/opposition. Check the domain — paid look-alikes exist. Link validity 15 vs 30 days (sources conflict): ask for a fresh link the week of the trip. **V/A**
- **Seller ≠ owner (lease LOA/LLD, common on 2023–25 BEVs):** if field **C.1** names a bank or lessor (captive finance arms, leasing companies), the seller is the lessee. He must buy the car out first; the lessor then bars the carte grise and signs the cession. Rule: C.4a = "est le propriétaire" in the seller's own name. Ask before flying: "Is the carte grise in your name, with no gage?" **V/A**
- C.1 must match the seller's ID (co-holders all sign); VIN (field E) must match windscreen + B-pillar; odometer ≥ last CT/HistoVec figure. **V**
- **No chèque de banque** (fakes common; impractical from a PT bank). **No deposit before seeing car + documents.** Pay SEPA Instant with Verification of Payee at the handover; papers + keys in the same sitting. **V/A**
- EU consumer warranty rights don't apply to private sales; manufacturer warranty stays with the car → [../coverage-rules.md](../coverage-rules.md). **V**
- "TVA récupérable" on a company seller's ad = ordinary VAT inside the gross price; final for a private buyer, no PT VAT on top ([README.md](README.md)). **V**

## 6. Buyer step list

**Before (remote)**
1. Photo of the carte grise (C.1, C.4a, VIN, first-registration date) + seller's ID.
2. HistoVec link: no gage/opposition, no VE/VGE, sensible owner count.
3. CSA <15 days old (or brought on the day).
4. Seller is the owner (no LOA/LLD) and has or will order the CoC.
5. Service/charging history; battery SoH evidence → [../battery-soh.md](../battery-soh.md).
6. Agree: SEPA Instant at handover; Cerfa 15776 filled on site; seller files the ANTS cession afterwards.
7. Buy drive insurance (A or B) **starting on the handover date, ≥3 weeks**; book the PT Category B inspection.
8. Book a one-way flight/train.

**Handover (~1 h)**
9. Inspect + test drive; check VIN plates.
10. Cerfa 15776 ×2 with date + time.
11. Transfer; wait for the seller to confirm receipt.
12. Seller bars the carte grise ("vendu le … à …h", signed) and hands over: carte grise, Cerfa copy 2, CSA, CoC, keys, cable, service book.

**After:** drive (2 days), keep entry-date proof; seller files ANTS cession ≤15 days; PT steps per [pt-registration.md](pt-registration.md).
