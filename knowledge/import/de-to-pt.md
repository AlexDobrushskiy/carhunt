# Germany → Portugal: seller-country side

> Snapshot: researched Oct–Nov 2026 (EV run). Prices, laws and tool behaviour drift — re-check anything you act on.

Tags: **V** verified (official/primary) · **A** anecdotal/forum/commercial · **I** inferred. The DE part (documents, export plate, office rules) applies to any destination; only the "covers the PT steps" parts are PT-specific. Destination side: [pt-registration.md](pt-registration.md). Contract (dealer order red flags, §312g withdrawal right) and payment: [../buy/contract-checklist.md](../buy/contract-checklist.md), [../buy/deposit-payment.md](../buy/deposit-payment.md).

**Difficulty: moderate.** One in-person office visit, same day possible. Timing is decided by three things: the export insurance card comes **by post** (order ~1 week ahead), the registration office (Zulassungsstelle) needs an **appointment** and is closed **Saturdays and Friday afternoons**, and many offices require a locally resident **authorised recipient (EBV)**.

## 1. Documents to collect (originals)

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil I (ZB I)** | Needed for de-registration and export plates. On export plates the old ZB I is withdrawn and replaced by an **export ZB I** with an expiry date (§45(3),(4) FZV). **V** |
| **ZB II (title), original** | Required for export plates (Berlin, Munich, Hamburg office pages). It is updated and **stays with the buyer**; de-registration doesn't touch it (§45(4), §16 FZV). You arrive with export ZB I + ZB II. **V** |
| **Kaufvertrag** (purchase contract) | Private sale: ADAC template; liability for defects can be excluded. Write in VIN (FIN) and the ZB II number and check both against the car. Must name the buyer exactly as on the ID (it is the PT DAV upload and the plate application). **V** (ADAC) |
| **HU report** (TÜV/DEKRA) | Must cover the **whole export-plate period**, or a new HU first (§45(1) FZV). First HU is due at 3 years — a car ~3 years old may need one. ~€163 avg HU+AU; EVs may be cheaper. **V** / cost **A** |
| **CoC** | Not required by DE for export plates on a DE-registered car, but **PT needs it** (IMT €45 with vs €165 without). Get it. **V** |
| De-registration confirmation, service record (digital for VW group), all keys/cards, charging cable | Contract items, not legal requirements |

## 2. Who de-registers, and when

- **The seller (registered keeper), online via i-Kfz, ~5 minutes**, any car last registered after 1 Jan 2015. He uncovers the security codes on the ZB I and plate seals; the plates become invalid at once. Fee **€2.10 online** vs €15.90 at the counter. **V** (Heidelberg, Karlsruhe office pages)
- **Only after the money has landed.** Don't let the seller remove the seals in advance. **V** (Karlsruhe)
- De-registering first is not mandatory — the office can convert the registration to export with the ZB I and the seller's plates (§45(4) FZV, Berlin) — but online de-registration is cleaner: the seller's insurance and liability end at once.

## 3. Transit: the export plate (Ausfuhrkennzeichen) — take 30 days

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Seller's plates + seller's insurance | Not plainly illegal (insurance passes with the sale, §95 VVG **V**), but the seller carries fines and no-claims damage, can't de-register later, and the car arrives still DE-registered. **Don't.** |
| **Kurzzeitkennzeichen (yellow 5-day plate)** | **Not workable:** valid to end of day 5 (§42(4) FZV); needs de-registration, valid HU, eVB, local EBV; national plate — **ADAC reports entry refused in FR, BE, NL and LU** with fines/seizure. Offices call export on it "auf eigenes Risiko". Insurance 5→6 days since 1 Mar 2024. **V** |
| **Ausfuhrkennzeichen (export plate)** ✅ | The legal tool for permanent export (§45 FZV). **V** |
| PT plate before leaving | Doesn't exist; PT issues plates only after the DAV. **V** |
| Truck | Car de-registered, travels plateless. €1,000–1,400 + 23% VAT open transporter (enclosed +€300–800); pickup 2–4 weeks after booking, transit 2–3 days, **3–5 weeks total**. Car sits paid-for at the seller's until pickup. **A** (PT importer page). In PT it must be delivered to the inspection centre or trailered there |

**Export plate details**

- **Validity = insurance period**, max 1 year, not backdatable (§45 FZV). Local caps: Hamburg normally 1 month, Münster max 30 days, Berlin 15 days–1 year. **V**
- **Take 30 days:** the clock starts at issue and must cover the drive (~2–3 days) **plus** the PT Category B inspection — see [pt-registration.md](pt-registration.md) §1. If it expires, PT presumes entry at expiry and the car can't be driven. (EV run 2026-11-04: 5- and 15-day options were quoted by forums; too short for PT's order.)
- **Insurance: paper card only** (Anlage 16 FZV, "gelbe Karte"), **sent by post**; no eVB for export plates (§49 FZV). **Order ~1 week ahead**; deliver to the seller or a hotel (posting abroad unconfirmed). The office won't fill in the card; incomplete card = no plate. **V**
- Example broker prices (zollplatz.de, AXA, liability + breakdown + legal, read **2026-11-04**): **9 days €75.42 · 15 days €76.93 · 30 days €87.64**; optional 30-day Vollkasko **€495**. FR, ES, PT need no green card. **V** (broker table)
- **Fees:** office €30–70 (Nürnberg €31.40 + €0.60–2.60, Munich from €31.80, Münster €47.40, Hamburg €30–70) **V**; plates €20–40 from a plate maker (usually next door) **A**. Kfz-Steuer **€0** for BEVs first registered up to 2030 (exempt to end-2035; Bundestag 4 Dec 2025, Bundesrat 19 Dec 2025) **V**, but offices still ask for a **SEPA direct-debit mandate** — a foreign IBAN works in Hamburg; without an EU account pay at the Hauptzollamt. **V**
- **In person, by appointment only** ("Nur nach Terminvereinbarung"); no i-Kfz route for export plates found. Bring the car (inspection only for never-DE-registered or doubtful cars). **V**
- **Opening hours: no Saturdays; Friday closing 12:00–13:30** (e.g. Stuttgart 12:00, Landkreis München 12:00, Köln 13:00, Berlin-Lichtenberg — Berlin's only export-plate office — 13:30). **Deal Thursday or very early Friday.** **V**
- **Foreign buyer without a German address:** you are the keeper on the export plate. Munich lets a non-resident staying there apply; Berlin, Hamburg, Münster, Aschaffenburg require an **EBV living in the district** (Hamburg: EBV at the counter in person); Heidelberg: the EBV's office is competent. **In practice ask the seller to be your EBV and come along.** **V**
- Power of attorney: informal, with both IDs (Berlin). No published fees for plate agencies serving foreign buyers; PT importers quote €150–300 incl. insurance as a service. **A**

## 5. DE-specific traps

- **No public lien register.** The lender keeps the **ZB II**. **No original ZB II ("the bank has it") = walk away** unless you get the bank's written release (Freigabe) and the payoff is paid first. Not checking the ZB II is gross negligence — you lose good-faith-buyer protection (BGH V ZR 148/21). **V**
- **Seller ID must match the keeper** on ZB I/II; a mismatch often means a dealer posing as private. **V** (ADAC checklist). ZB II field (1) gives the number of previous keepers.
- **VIN** on the car = ZB I = contract, or don't sign. **V**
- No police stolen-car lookup for private buyers; commercial VIN reports query police data but an empty result is no guarantee. **A**
- **HU timing:** a car near 3 years old may have its first HU due inside the plate period — ask the seller to do it before the sale.
- Cash ≥€10k must be declared only at the EU external border; avoid cash anyway. **V**
- **Dealer purchase:** VAT on the invoice is final (no PT VAT). Distance-sale withdrawal right, handover-protocol-only and order red flags → [../buy/contract-checklist.md](../buy/contract-checklist.md). The final invoice must carry the buyer's country. (EV run 2026-11-07)
- Winter: in DE winter conditions all wheels need snowflake-marked tyres (§2(3a) StVO) — drive home on a matched set ([pt-registration.md](pt-registration.md) §8).

## 6. Buyer step list

**Before (D-10 to D-3)**
1. Get photos of ZB I + ZB II (both sides) + seller ID (name visible), VIN, HU date, CoC (PDF or original), service history, ZB II number, and whether the car is financed.
2. Seller confirms: HU done if due within 30 days; acts as your **EBV** and comes to the office; de-registers **only after the money lands**.
3. Book the Zulassungsstelle appointment for export plates in the seller's district — **Thursday or Friday 08:00**. Download the application and EBV forms.
4. **Order the 30-day export insurance** now (paper card by post), with VIN + your details, delivered to the seller or hotel.
5. Book the PT Category B inspection (see [pt-registration.md](pt-registration.md)).

**Deal day**
6. Inspect, check VIN, match the ZB II original to the seller's ID, sign the contract (2 copies).
7. Instant transfer with Verification of Payee → seller de-registers online (€2.10) → hands over ZB I, ZB II, keys, CoC, de-registration confirmation.
8. 🏢 Office with the seller (EBV): passport/ID, driving licence, ZB I + ZB II, insurance card, HU report, SEPA mandate, EBV form (+ seller's plates if not de-registered). ~30–60 min + slot.
9. Plate maker → mount plates (they show the expiry date) → drive. Carry the export ZB I + insurance card.

**After:** drive home (2 nights), keep entry-date proof, then the PT steps before the plate expires.
